❓ Frequently Asked Questions
Is breakfast included in the room rate?+
Breakfast depends on your selected rate. You can check if it is included during the final steps of booking or in your confirmation email.
What payment methods do you accept?+
We accept major credit cards and cash. A valid credit card is also required at check-in for incidental deposits.
What is your cancellation policy?+
Free cancellation is typically available up to a specific number of days before arrival if you choose a flexible rate. Non-refundable rates cannot be changed or refunded.
What time are check-in and check-out?+
Standard check-in begins at 3:00 PM, and check-out is required by 11:00 AM.
Can I request a late check-out?+
Late check-out may be available upon request at the front desk, subject to room availability and an extra fee.
Can I store my luggage after checking out?+
Complimentary luggage storage is available at the front desk for guests on their departure day.
Do you provide free Wi-Fi?+
High-speed wireless internet is free and accessible in all guest rooms and public areas.
Are toiletries and hair dryers provided?+
Complimentary shampoo, soap, and bath towels are stocked in every room bathroom.
Is parking available on-site?+
Secure on-site parking is available for registered guests.
